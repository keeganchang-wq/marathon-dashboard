🧠 Race Mantras & Mental Notes
🏁 Race Course Notes
🏃 Half Marathon Course
Start/Finish: Batman Avenue, Melbourne
Surface: 100% road, mostly flat
Key points:
· Km 0–3: Along the Yarra — easy start, don't get caught up in the crowd pace
· Km 3–7: St Kilda Road stretch — long and straight, find your rhythm
· Km 7–14: Beaconsfield Parade — coastal section, possible headwind
· Km 14–18: Return via Albert Park — this is where you race
· Km 18–21: Final push back to city — everything you've got
⚠ Wind: The St Kilda coastal section (km 7–14) often has headwind. Tuck behind a group. Don't fight it alone.
🏆 Full Marathon Course
Start/Finish: Batman Avenue, Melbourne
Surface: 100% road, mostly flat, one hill
Key points:
· Km 0–10: City loop — conservative, bank time by NOT going fast
· Km 10–21: Same as half course — St Kilda, coastal
· Km 21–30: Return loop via Albert Park Lake — stay controlled
· Km 30–35: The Tan / Botanical Gardens — THE HARD PART
· Km 35–42: Final city stretch — race it home
⚠ The Wall: Km 30–35 is where the marathon starts. Everything before is just getting there. Stay fuelled. One km at a time.
